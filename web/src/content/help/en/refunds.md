Every refund goes to **your original payment method** through Stripe. You can see the status of a return and the expected refund in the app under **Profile → Returns**.

## When you get the full amount back

- You cancelled the order before the shop accepted it.
- The shop did not accept the order or could not fulfil it.
- No courier was found.
- You did not collect a Reserve & collect order in time.
- The delivery was substantially late and you cancelled it.

## Refunds for returns

- You return **the whole order**: you get back the price of the items and the original delivery fee, and any other fees, less any discount.
- You return **only some items**: you get back their price, less the matching share of any discount code. The delivery fee and other fees are refunded once the whole order has been returned. Returning in parts adds up to the same amount as returning everything at once.
- The refund is paid **within 14 days** of your withdrawal. It may wait until the item is back with the seller.

## When a delivery failed

If the courier could not reach you and took the order back to the shop, you get the price of the items back (less any discount). The delivery fee, the tip and other fees are not refunded, unless the failure was our fault, the shop's or the courier's.

## When the money shows on your account

Once a refund is made, how quickly it shows depends on your bank and card issuer, and it can take a few banking days. If your refund doesn't arrive, [contact us](/en/help/contact/) and choose the topic **Returns and refunds**.
