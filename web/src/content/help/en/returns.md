## From business sellers: 14 days

When you buy from a **business**, you can withdraw from the purchase **within 14 days of receiving the item**, without giving a reason (Finnish Consumer Protection Act, chapter 6).

1. Open **Profile → Returns**, or the order in the **Orders** tab.
2. Choose whether you return **the whole order or only some items**, and add details if you like.
3. The app shows the shop's name, address and contact details, so you can take the item back to the shop.

You can also tell us you are withdrawing in any other clear way. Unless the seller has agreed otherwise, you pay the cost of sending the item back.

A seller may make a deduction only if the item has lost value because it was handled more than needed to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning. Any deduction is explained to you in writing.

## Hygiene-sealed items

Some cosmetics, fragrances, and piercing jewellery or earrings are delivered **sealed for health and hygiene reasons**. The product page, your bag and your order confirmation say which items these are.

- An **unopened** sealed item can be returned within 14 days like anything else.
- **Once the seal is opened**, the right of withdrawal no longer applies (Consumer Protection Act 6:16). When you return a sealed item, the app asks whether the seal is unbroken.
- Clothing, including underwear and swimwear, is never sold under this rule.

## Faulty items

A faulty item, or one that is not as described, can **always** be returned, also when it is sealed and also after 14 days. You have the rights of the Consumer Protection Act, chapter 5: repair, replacement, a price reduction or cancelling the purchase.

## From private sellers

The legal right of withdrawal **does not apply** to items bought from private individuals. You can still send a return request in the app, and the seller decides. Every shop page says whether the seller is a business or a private person.

## Recalls

If a product you bought is recalled for safety reasons, we tell you directly by notification and email. Recalls are also published on the [recalls page](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/recalls).
