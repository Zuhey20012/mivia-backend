We collect only what is needed to buy, sell and deliver. Your data is on our server in Frankfurt (EU), and all traffic is encrypted. The full explanation of every piece of data – why we need it, who sees it, how long we keep it and how you control it: **[Your data, explained](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/data-explained)**.

## Shoppers

- **Account:** name, email, a phone number if you give one, and your password (only as a hash). The store does not see your name, email or phone number.
- **Delivery address and map pin:** so we can show nearby stores and the right delivery fee, and the courier can find you. Saved addresses stay on your phone. "Use my location" reads your position once, only when you tap it.
- **Orders and returns:** kept 6 years because of the Accounting Act, without your name once you delete your account.
- **Chat with the courier:** deleted 30 days after the delivery.
- **Delivery photo:** only when the parcel is left at your door; seen only by you, the courier and Malvoya, and deleted after 30 days.
- **Drops, comments and live chat:** likes, saves and followed stores until you remove them; live chat 30 days.

## Shops and sellers

- **Public store profile:** the name, address, map position and business ID are visible to everyone. A home-based store's address may be a home address.
- **Tax details (DAC7):** required by law. The tax ID is encrypted, and the details are kept 10 years.
- **Payouts:** you give bank and identity details on Stripe's pages, not to Malvoya.

## Couriers

- **Location:** only while you are online or delivering. Only the latest position is stored. It is deleted when you go offline, or after 15 minutes without an update (for example if the app is closed). There is no route history.
- **Jobs and pay:** you see the pay breakdown of every job.

## What we never do

- We never sell your data and show no ads.
- No session recording and no third-party analytics.
- No card numbers on our servers: Stripe handles payments.
- No profiling of minors.

## How you control your data

In the Malvoya app: **Profile → Privacy → Your data** – download your data, email preferences and account deletion. In Malvoya Store and Courier you can delete your account from the account menu; ask Help for a copy of your data. You can also complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en)).
