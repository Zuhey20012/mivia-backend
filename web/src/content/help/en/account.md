## Signing in

You can sign in with **email and password**, with **Google** or with a **one-time code**. You can also browse without an account; you need one to pay, follow your orders and save favourites. Malvoya is for people aged 18 or over.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and type your account's email address.
2. You get a **6-digit code** by email. It is valid for 15 minutes.
3. Enter the code and choose a new password.

When the password changes, **every other device is signed out**. If your account was made with Google or a one-time code, it doesn't need a password, and the email tells you so.

For your security, we always say the code was sent, whether or not the address has an account.

## Changing your password

When you are signed in, you can change your password in the account settings. You need your current password. Other devices are signed out.

## Linking Google

If you made your account with email and password, you can link a Google account to it in the account settings. After that you can also sign in with Google.

## Language

Change the language under **Profile → Language**. Notifications and emails come in the language you choose.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time: **Profile → Delete account** (or **Profile → Your data**). It takes effect at once. If you have an order in progress, finish or cancel it first. What is deleted, and what the law requires us to keep, is explained on the [account deletion page](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/delete-account).
