## Download your data

1. Open **Profile → Your data** (Download or delete your data).
2. Tap **Download your data**.
3. Choose **Copy** or **Email it to me**.

You get your data in a machine-readable format: your account details, your orders and the other data stored with your account, including your waiting-list alert.

## Delete your account

Under **Profile → Your data** you can also delete your account. It takes effect at once. Your name, contact details, password, favourites, comments and messages are deleted. **Order records** are kept, anonymised, for the six years the Finnish Accounting Act requires, and are no longer linked to you. More: [Delete your account](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/delete-account).

## What we don't do

- **No ads and no ad tracking.** There are no analytics trackers in the app.
- We don't profile you: Drops are ordered by how new they are, reactions, distance and the shop's rating, and you can choose the chronological order instead.
- Malvoya never sees your card number; payments are handled by Stripe.
- Our servers are in the EU (Frankfurt).

## Location and photos

- You see the courier's location only during your own delivery.
- The photo of a parcel left at your door is seen only by you, the courier and Malvoya, and is deleted after 30 days.

## Questions and complaints

Ask about your data in the app: **Profile → Help → Contact us**, topic **Account and privacy**. We reply by email. You can also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman ([tietosuoja.fi](https://tietosuoja.fi/en)).
