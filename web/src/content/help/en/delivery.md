## Delivery to your door

- Once the shop has accepted your order and marked it ready, a **local courier** picks it up at the shop and brings it to you.
- The app shows the estimated delivery time. You can **follow the courier on a map** and message them about the delivery.
- The delivery fee depends on the distance between the shop and your address. You see it before you pay.
- The courier hands the order to you, or leaves it at the door and takes a photo of the parcel. Only you, the courier and Malvoya can see the photo, and it is deleted after 30 days.

### If the delivery fails

If the courier cannot reach you at the address, they wait 10 minutes and then take the order back to the shop. The order is cancelled and you get **the price of the items** back. The delivery fee, the tip and any other fees are not refunded in that case, because the delivery was made and the courier is paid for it. If it was our fault, the shop's or the courier's (for example the courier went to the wrong address), you get everything back: [contact us](/en/help/contact/).

If a delivery is substantially late, you can cancel and get the full amount back.

## Reserve & collect

- Choose **Reserve & collect** at checkout. There is no delivery fee.
- You get a notification when the order is ready to collect. The app shows the shop's address.
- Show your **6-digit pickup code** at the shop. Only you see the code, and the shop hands the order over only after it has entered the right code.
- If you don't collect the order in time, it is cancelled and the full amount is refunded. We remind you in the app before that.

Malvoya is opening one area at a time, so not every shop delivers everywhere yet. The app only shows shops that deliver to your address.
