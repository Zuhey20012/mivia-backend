## Finding shops and products

1. Open Malvoya and choose your **delivery address**. The app shows the shops near you, how far they are, the delivery fee and whether they are open right now.
2. Pick **All, Women, Men or Kids** at the top, then browse the categories, search, or shop from the shops' **Drops**.
3. A product page shows photos, any videos, sizes and options, and what is available. Beauty products also show the unit price.

Every shop page says who the seller is and whether it is a **business or a private seller**. That matters for returns: see [Returns](/en/help/returns/).

## At checkout

- You see the **whole price before you pay**: the items, the delivery fee, any other fees and your discount code. You pay exactly the amount you see.
- Choose **delivery** or **Reserve & collect** (pick it up at the shop, with no delivery fee).
- You can add an optional tip for the courier. All of it goes to the courier.
- Payments are handled by **Stripe**. Malvoya never sees your card number.

You need an account to pay. You can browse without one, and your bag stays as it is when you sign in.

## After you order

- The shop accepts your order within minutes. If the shop does not accept it, or no courier is found, **the order is cancelled and the full amount is refunded automatically**.
- You can cancel the order yourself in the app until the shop accepts it. You then get the full amount back.
- If you left a payment unfinished, the unpaid checkout is cancelled after 30 minutes and you are not charged.

Follow your order in the **Orders** tab. More: [Delivery and pickup](/en/help/delivery-and-pickup/).
