## In the app

1. Open **Profile → Help → Contact us** (Send a message to the Malvoya team).
2. Choose a topic:
   - **Orders and delivery** – where your order is, a missing or wrong item
   - **Returns and refunds**
   - **Payments** – charges, declined payments and receipts
   - **Account and privacy** – signing in, your details and your data
   - **Selling or delivering** – opening a shop or becoming a courier
   - **Something else**
3. Write your message. We reply by email.

When your message is about an order, include the order number so we can find it quickly.

## Help assistant

**Profile → Help → Help assistant** gives automatic answers to common questions about orders, returns and sizes. It is not a person; if it doesn't help, choose Contact us.

## During a delivery

You can message your courier directly from the order screen.

## For shops and couriers

The Malvoya Store and Malvoya Courier apps have their own help and contact options. About joining: [For shops](/en/sell/) and [Deliver with Malvoya](/en/deliver/).

If you can't open the app or sign in, the [account deletion page](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/delete-account) explains how to ask for help.
