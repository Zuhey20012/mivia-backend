## 4. Prices and payment

- All prices are in euros and include VAT where the seller is VAT-registered.
- The total price, including the delivery fee, is shown before you pay.
- Malvoya may charge a service fee or a small-order fee (for orders below a set amount). Any such fee is shown as its own line, and included in the total, before you pay. You never pay more than the total shown at checkout.
- **Promo codes.** A promo code gives the discount described with it, under its stated conditions (for example a minimum order or first order only). Unless the code says otherwise, each customer can use a code once. A code cannot be exchanged for cash. Malvoya pays the discount; the seller and the courier are paid in full. Malvoya can end a campaign at any time, but a discount on an order you have already paid is honoured. If your order is cancelled before delivery, you can use the code again.
- You can add an optional tip for the courier at checkout. The whole tip goes to the courier. If the order is cancelled, the tip is refunded with the rest of the payment, except in the case described in section 5a (the courier could not reach you at the address).
- Payment is processed by Stripe. The order is confirmed only after the payment has been confirmed.
- Malvoya never sees your card or bank details.

## 5. Drops, reviews and reports

- **Drops** are short videos and photos posted by stores. How the feed is ranked is explained in the app ("Why am I seeing this?") and in the Privacy Policy. You can switch to the chronological "Latest" view.
- **Reviews** can only be written by customers whose order was delivered, once per order, within 30 days. We do not edit reviews or pay for them. Reviews that break the law are hidden, which also removes them from the store's rating.
- **Reporting:** you can report any drop that you think is illegal or breaks these terms, also without an account. A person reviews every report. Three reports from different users hide the drop until it has been reviewed. You can also report a comment or a review; a person on the Malvoya team decides whether to hide it.
- **Blocking:** you can block another user. Their comments and reviews are then hidden for you. They are not told. You can unblock them at any time.

## 5a. Delivery

The estimated delivery time is shown in the app. If the courier leaves the order at your door, they take a photo that only you, the courier and Malvoya can see; it is deleted after 30 days. If a delivery cannot be completed, we will contact you. If the delay is substantial, you may cancel and get a full refund.

You can chat with your courier in the app while the order is on its way. The messages are kept for 30 days after the order is completed or cancelled, then deleted.

**If the courier cannot reach you at the address.** Please be available when your order arrives, and give an address and details the courier can find. If the courier is at your address and cannot reach you (or cannot find the address), the app tells you at once and asks you to reply in the chat. The courier waits at least 10 minutes. If you still cannot be reached, the courier takes the order back to the shop and the order is cancelled. In that case:

- we refund what you paid for the items (after any discount) to your original payment method;
- the delivery fee, the courier's tip and any service or small-order fee are **not** refunded, because the delivery was made and the courier is paid for it;
- you are told in the app and by email.

This does not apply if the failed delivery was our, the store's or the courier's fault (for example the courier went to the wrong address). Then you get a full refund; contact us if that happened.

## 5b. Reserve & collect (pickup from the store)

- Some stores let you reserve items in the app and collect them from the store yourself. There is no delivery fee and no courier, so you cannot add a tip. Any service fee, small-order fee or promo code works as for delivery. You can place a pickup order only while the store is open.
- You pay in the app when you order. The store accepts the order and tells you in the app when it is ready. The store address is shown before you pay.
- Collect the order within **[72] hours** of the "ready for pickup" message. Show the 6-digit pickup code from the app at the store; the store hands the order over only against that code. Do not share the code with anyone you do not want to collect the order for you.
- If you do not collect the order in time, it is cancelled and the full amount is refunded to your original payment method. We remind you in the app before that.
- When you buy from a **business seller**, your 14-day right of withdrawal (section 7) counts from the day you collect the order.

## 6. Cancelling before the store accepts

You can cancel an order in the app until the store accepts it. The full amount is refunded.

A checkout you did not pay for is cancelled automatically after 30 minutes, or as soon as you start a new checkout; you are not charged for it. If the store cannot fulfil your order, it cancels it, tells you why, and the full amount is refunded.

## 7. Right of withdrawal (14 days) — business sellers

- You may withdraw from a purchase within **14 days of receiving the item**, without giving a reason (Consumer Protection Act, chapter 6).
- Start the return in the app (Profile → Returns) or send an unambiguous statement to [support@malvoya.com].
- We refund the price and the original standard delivery fee (and any service or small-order fee, less a promo discount) within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal. The refund goes to your original payment method.
- You can also return only some of the items. Then we refund the price of those items, less the matching share of any promo discount. The delivery fee and any service or small-order fee are refunded when the whole order has been returned.
- A refund may be delayed until the item has been received back.
- You pay the cost of returning the item unless the seller has agreed otherwise [OR: "Malvoya arranges and pays for courier pick-up"].
- A deduction may be made only if the item has lost value because it was handled beyond what is necessary to establish its nature, characteristics and functioning. Any deduction is explained to you in writing.

## 8. Defects

If an item is defective or not as described, you have the rights of the Consumer Protection Act, chapter 5 (repair, replacement, price reduction or cancellation), regardless of the withdrawal period.

- Malvoya can change or end the programme at any time. Reward codes already issued stay valid under their own conditions.
- Misuse, for example fake or duplicate accounts, inviting yourself or publishing codes in a misleading way, voids the discount and the reward, and we may withdraw codes obtained this way.
