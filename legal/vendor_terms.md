# Malvoya Seller Terms / Myyjän ehdot

**Last updated:** [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

> **Before publishing:** have these terms reviewed by a lawyer. They are written to meet the EU Platform-to-Business Regulation (2019/1150), the Digital Services Act and the DAC7 reporting rules.

## 1. Parties

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (Y-tunnus [Y-TUNNUS]) ("Malvoya") and the seller who registers a store ("Seller").

## 2. Seller identity and status

- Business sellers provide their company name, Y-tunnus, address and VAT status.
- Private individuals selling their own used items provide their name, address and date of birth.
- Customers are shown whether each store is a **business** or a **private seller**, as marketplace law requires.
- For DAC7, Malvoya collects each seller's tax identification details and reports sellers who exceed the thresholds to the Finnish Tax Administration each year.
- A store becomes visible to customers only after Malvoya has reviewed these details.

## 3. Listings

The Seller is responsible for accurate descriptions, prices including VAT, product safety and the legality of the items. Malvoya may remove unlawful listings, and will tell the Seller why.

The Seller may import listings from their own online shop or a CSV file. Before each import the Seller confirms that they may use the photos and texts (for example their own material or material the brand has authorised). Photos are copied into Malvoya's storage for the Seller's store, and imported listings stay hidden until the Seller has checked and published them.

## 4. Orders

- Orders are shown to the Seller only after the customer's payment is confirmed.
- The Seller accepts or declines each order. Declined orders are refunded to the customer automatically.
- Accepted orders are prepared promptly for courier pickup.
- **Reserve & collect.** If the customer chose to collect the order from the store, the Seller marks the order ready in the app once it is packed. The Seller hands the order over only after entering the customer's 6-digit pickup code in the app; the code is shown to the customer only. Five wrong codes lock the handover for 15 minutes. A correct code completes the order; it counts as delivered, which starts the customer's withdrawal period and the payout schedule in section 6. An order that is not collected within [72] hours of being marked ready is cancelled and refunded to the customer, and the Seller puts the items back on sale. The Seller keeps reserved items safe until they are collected or the order is cancelled.

## 5. Returns

Business sellers must honour the customer's 14-day right of withdrawal and defect rights under the Consumer Protection Act. Returns are processed in the app. Any deduction for diminished value must be justified in writing.

## 6. Fees and payouts

- Commission: [X] % of the item price (excluding the delivery fee), unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- Payouts are made through Stripe Connect to the Seller's own Stripe account, 15 days after delivery (after the 14-day return window), with refunds and commission deducted. Stripe verifies the Seller's identity and bank account on its own pages.
- Malvoya provides a statement for each payout.

## 6a. Drops, photos and videos

The Seller may post short videos and photos ("drops") of their own products. The Seller must hold the rights to everything shown (music, logos, people). Malvoya may remove drops that are illegal or break these terms and always tells the Seller why and how to object (Digital Services Act Art. 17).

## 7. Ranking

Stores are listed by distance to the customer (when location is shared) and by rating. Malvoya does not sell better placement. [UPDATE IF PAID PLACEMENT IS INTRODUCED.]

## 8. Suspension and ending the agreement

- Malvoya gives 30 days' notice before ending the agreement, and immediate suspension only for serious breach or unlawful content.
- Reasons are always given in writing, and the Seller can use the internal complaint process at [sellers@malvoya.com].
- Mediation is available through [MEDIATOR].

## 9. Law

These terms are governed by Finnish law.
