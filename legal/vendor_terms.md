## 2. Seller identity and status

- Business sellers provide their company name, Y-tunnus, address and VAT status.
- Private individuals selling their own used items provide their name, address and date of birth.
- Customers are shown whether each store is a **business** or a **private seller**, as marketplace law requires.
- **Tax details (DAC7).** EU law (Council Directive (EU) 2021/514, "DAC7") requires Malvoya to collect, check and report each seller's details to the Finnish Tax Administration once a year, by 31 January for the previous year. The Seller gives them in the store app (Store → Tax details):
  - business sellers: legal name, business ID (Y-tunnus), VAT number if registered, and registered address with country;
  - private sellers: legal name, date of birth, tax identification number (for Finnish residents the personal identity code, henkilötunnus) and home address with country.
- Malvoya reports, per seller, these details, the number of sales in the year, and per quarter the amounts paid or credited to the Seller (sales less refunds and Malvoya's commission) and the commission Malvoya withheld. Sellers with fewer than 30 sales and at most 2 000 € in the year are not reported. Malvoya keeps the details and report data for 10 years after the year concerned, also if the Seller closes their store or deletes their account. The tax identification number is stored encrypted and shown to the Seller only in part; only Malvoya's administrators can see it in full, for the report. The Seller keeps the details up to date. If a store with sales has not given its details, Malvoya reminds the Seller in the app and by email, at most every 30 days and twice a year. Malvoya may withhold payouts or suspend a store whose details are missing or wrong after two reminders, as the directive requires; this is a decision made by a person, never automatically.
- **Business ID check.** When a business seller enters its Y-tunnus, Malvoya checks its form and check digit and looks it up in the Finnish Patent and Registration Office's free open company register (YTJ): whether it exists, the registered name, the registration date and whether the company is active. The result helps Malvoya's team review the store; it never approves or rejects a store by itself. Private sellers have no Y-tunnus and skip this check.
- The store app shows a checklist of what is still needed before review (store details, map location, opening hours, payouts, a first product, tax details, these terms, the product safety pledge, and for businesses the business ID check). When everything is done, Malvoya's team is told that the store is ready for review.
- A store becomes visible to customers only after a person at Malvoya has reviewed these details.

## 3. Listings

The Seller is responsible for accurate descriptions, prices including VAT, product safety and the legality of the items. Malvoya may remove unlawful listings, and will tell the Seller why.

### 3a. Product safety

- **Pledge.** Before publishing a first product the Seller confirms once in the app that it only offers products that comply with EU and Finnish law, including the General Product Safety Regulation (EU) 2023/988, and that it acts on recalls and safety warnings without delay. The time and the version of the pledge are recorded.
- **Distributor or manufacturer.** A Seller that sells products made by others acts as a distributor: before offering a product it checks that the product carries the manufacturer's details, a type or other identifier and the instructions and safety information in Finnish and Swedish, and it does not offer products it knows or should know are dangerous. A Seller that sells under its own name or brand, makes products itself (for example handmade goods) or changes them is the manufacturer and has the manufacturer's duties. For cosmetics sold under its own name it is the responsible person under the Cosmetics Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009 (product information file, safety assessment, CPNP notification).
- **Manufacturer and EU responsible person.** For every product a business Seller gives the manufacturer's name, postal address and an email address or website, and, when the manufacturer is outside the EU/EEA, the same details of the responsible person in the EU. The Seller can save these as records and reuse them.
- **Recalls.** If Malvoya learns that a product is dangerous or subject to a recall or safety warning (from an authority, the manufacturer, the Seller or a notice), Malvoya may lock the listing and all identical listings (same EAN/GTIN, or same brand and model) so they cannot be shown again, tell the Seller why, publish the recall on its recalls page and notify every customer who bought the product directly. The Seller cooperates with the recall and with the authorities, does not sell the recalled product again, and offers the customers the remedies of the recall (at least two of repair, replacement or refund, free of charge, where the Seller is responsible for the recall). Malvoya may refund customers on the Seller's behalf through Stripe and deduct the refund from the Seller's payouts.
- **Reports.** Anyone can report a product in the app (unsafe, counterfeit, illegal, misleading or other). Malvoya's team reviews product-safety notices within three working days; if it removes a listing it tells the Seller why, and the Seller may ask for the decision to be reviewed.
- **Repeat offenders.** Malvoya may suspend, after a warning, a Seller that repeatedly offers non-compliant or dangerous products.

### 3b. Required product information

Before a product can be published the app asks for the information the law requires for that kind of product, and refuses to publish it until it is complete. Drafts may be incomplete. In particular:
- **Textiles** (clothing, textile accessories, textile bags): the fibre composition with percentages, using only the fibre names of Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011, and "Contains non-textile parts of animal origin" when relevant. A private person selling their own second-hand item may say the label is missing.
- **Footwear:** the material of the upper, the lining and insole, and the outer sole.
- **Cosmetics, skincare, haircare and fragrance:** the content (ml or g), the shelf life (best-before date or period after opening), the function and the precautions in Finnish and Swedish, the ingredient list (INCI) and the cosmetics responsible person in the EU. Only new, sealed and unused products, never testers or samples or professional-use-only products. At launch only business sellers may sell these products.
- **Jewellery** (new items, business sellers): how the nickel release is known to be within the limit, and for non-precious metals how lead and cadmium are known to be within the limits (REACH Annex XVII).
- **Sunglasses:** the UV filter category and CE marking. **Electrical hair tools:** CE marking and the safety instructions in Finnish and Swedish.
- **Children's clothing:** no cords or drawstrings in the hood or neck area for children under 7 (EN 14682), and sizes in centimetres.
- **Hygiene seal:** the Seller may mark only genuinely sealed cosmetics, fragrance and piercing jewellery or earrings in sealed packs as hygiene-sealed; customers cannot withdraw from those purchases once the seal is opened. Clothing, including underwear and swimwear, is never hygiene-sealed.
- The unit price (per litre or per kilogram) is calculated by Malvoya from the content the Seller gives; the Seller must give the correct nominal content.
- The Seller must keep the information correct and up to date, and copy it from the product's own label.

### 3c. Environmental and other claims, certificates

- The Seller must not use generic environmental claims such as "eco", "ekologinen", "environmentally friendly", "ympäristöystävällinen", "sustainable", "vastuullinen", "climate neutral" or "biodegradable" in names, descriptions, tags or photos, and must not show sustainability labels that are not based on a certification scheme (Directive (EU) 2024/825, Consumer Protection Act). The app flags such words and does not publish a listing that contains them. Specific, verifiable facts are allowed (for example "60 % recycled polyester, GRS certified").
- Certificates (for example EU Ecolabel, Nordic Swan, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, Fairtrade, GRS, Leaping Bunny, The Vegan Society) are shown only after Malvoya's team has checked the licence the Seller submitted, only for the products and the part of the product they cover, and only until they expire. The Seller gives the licence number, the licence holder, what it covers, its expiry date and a copy of the certificate, and tells Malvoya at once if a certificate is withdrawn.
- Claims about origin ("Made in Finland") and handmade goods must be true; the Seller confirms them in the app and shows evidence on request.
- The Seller must keep evidence of every claim it makes and provide it to Malvoya or an authority on request.

The Seller may import listings from their own online shop or a CSV file. Before each import the Seller confirms that they may use the photos and texts (for example their own material or material the brand has authorised). Photos are copied into Malvoya's storage for the Seller's store, and imported listings stay hidden until the Seller has checked and published them. Imported products are sorted into Malvoya's categories automatically; the Seller checks the category and adds the required product information (section 3b) before publishing. Products Malvoya does not sell (for example candles, home décor, food supplements, medicines, hand sanitisers and nail glue) are held and cannot be published.

## 4. Orders

- Orders are shown to the Seller only after the customer's payment is confirmed.
- The Seller accepts or declines each order. Declined orders are refunded to the customer automatically. When the Seller declines or cancels an order it can give a short reason, which is shown to the customer.
- Accepted orders are prepared promptly for courier pickup.
- **Customer not reached.** If the courier cannot reach the customer at the address, the courier waits 10 minutes and then brings the order back to the store. The Seller takes the items back at the counter and confirms it in the app. The order is cancelled, the items return to the Seller's stock and the customer gets the item price back; the Seller receives no payout and no commission is charged for that order.
- **Scheduled delivery.** When Malvoya offers delivery windows in the app, a customer may book one for today or tomorrow that starts while the store is open. The Seller accepts the order as usual (within 10 minutes of payment) and has it ready by the start of the slot; couriers are offered the job shortly before then (the Seller's preparation time plus 15 minutes). Any discount on the delivery fee for a scheduled slot is paid by Malvoya and does not change the Seller's payout.
- **Reserve & collect.** If the customer chose to collect the order from the store, the Seller marks the order ready in the app once it is packed. The Seller hands the order over only after entering the customer's 6-digit pickup code in the app; the code is shown to the customer only. Five wrong codes lock the handover for 15 minutes. A correct code completes the order; it counts as delivered, which starts the customer's withdrawal period and the payout schedule in section 6. An order that is not collected within [72] hours of being marked ready is cancelled and refunded to the customer, and the Seller puts the items back on sale. The Seller keeps reserved items safe until they are collected or the order is cancelled.

## 5. Returns

Business sellers must honour the customer's 14-day right of withdrawal and defect rights under the Consumer Protection Act. Returns are processed in the app. A hygiene-sealed item (section 3b) whose seal the customer says is opened cannot be returned under the right of withdrawal; the Seller may reject such a return on receipt if the seal is in fact broken, with the reason. Faulty items can always be returned. Any deduction for diminished value must be justified in writing. When the Seller rejects a return it must give the reason, which is shown to the customer.

## 6. Fees and payouts

- Commission: [X] % of the item price (excluding the delivery fee), unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- Payouts are made through Stripe Connect to the Seller's own Stripe account, 15 days after delivery (after the 14-day return window), with refunds and commission deducted. Stripe verifies the Seller's identity and bank account on its own pages.
- Malvoya provides a monthly statement in the store app listing each order delivered or collected in the month with its sales, refunds, commission and payout, and the month's totals (it can be printed). The statement is a summary for the Seller's records, not a VAT invoice. Discounts from Malvoya's promo and invite codes are paid by Malvoya and do not reduce the Seller's sales or payout.

## 6a. Drops, photos and videos

The Seller may post short videos and photos ("drops") of their own products. The Seller must hold the rights to everything shown (music, logos, people). Malvoya may remove drops that are illegal or break these terms and always tells the Seller why and how to object (Digital Services Act Art. 17).

## 7a. Accepting these terms and changes to them

- The Seller accepts these terms when creating the store account or in the store app. Malvoya records which version was accepted, when, how, the network address shortened to its first part (IPv4: first three of four numbers; IPv6: first three of eight groups), the app or browser type and the app version, and emails the Seller a copy of the full text accepted. The copy can be opened in the store app at any time.
- Every change is published as a new version with a summary of what changes and the date it takes effect. Malvoya tells the Seller by push notification and email, in the Seller's language, with a link to the full new text. All versions stay readable at [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/sellers/versions](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/sellers/versions).
- A change takes effect **no earlier than 15 days** after it is announced (EU Platform-to-Business Regulation 2019/1150, Art. 3), unless the law requires Malvoya to make the change sooner; the notice then says so and why. Malvoya may give longer notice when a change needs more work from sellers.
- **The Seller may end the agreement before a change takes effect**, free of charge, by closing the store (store app → Account → Delete account) or by telling Malvoya support. Orders already accepted are completed and paid out under the terms that applied to them.
- Once a new version is in effect, the store app asks the Seller to accept it before accepting new orders or publishing products. Until then the Seller can still sign in, see and finish orders already accepted, handle returns, receive payouts, see statements, contact support, export data and close the store.
