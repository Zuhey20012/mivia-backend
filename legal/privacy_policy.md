### Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When a Malvoya app crashes, or a request fails because of a fault on our side, the app sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics, which processes it for us (section 5). A report holds the technical details of the fault (the error, where in the app it happened, which app and app version), information about the device (such as model, operating system version and free memory), a random identifier of the app installation and, if you are signed in, your numeric Malvoya user id. It never holds your name, email address, phone number, address, messages or what you typed; error texts are cleaned of such details before they are sent. Development builds of the apps send no reports.

- **Purpose:** finding and fixing faults in the apps.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in apps that work reliably and securely (6(1)(f)). You can object (section 8).
- **Retention:** Firebase keeps crash reports for 90 days and then deletes them automatically.

## 3. Location data

- **Customers:** we use the delivery address and the map pin you choose. If you tap "Use my location" when choosing the pin, the app reads your phone's position once, only to place the pin; nothing is sent until you confirm the address. The pin is sent with requests to show nearby stores, delivery fees and delivery times, and it is stored only with an order you place (and removed from the order 30 days after it was delivered or cancelled) or with a "tell me when Malvoya opens near me" alert. Your saved addresses stay on your phone. The app does not track you in the background.
- **Stores:** if the store owner taps "Use my current location" when setting up the store, the app reads the phone's position once to place the store's pin.
- **Couriers:** while you are online or delivering, your position is shared with the customer and store of the order you are delivering, from when you accept the job until it is delivered or taken back to the shop; it is never shown on finished or cancelled orders. We keep only your latest position, and it is deleted as soon as you go offline, or automatically when no update has arrived for 15 minutes (you are then set offline; a delivery in progress carries on). We do not store a history of your routes. Two things are kept with a job: the distance of your ride to the store when you accept it (for your pay) and the position where you hand an order over (proof of delivery, for every handover, deleted after 30 days).
- **Maps and address search:** the map pictures and the address search in the apps come straight from OpenStreetMap's servers (section 5), which therefore see your phone's network address, the map area you look at and the addresses you search for.

## 4. Automated decisions and recommendations

**Drops feed.** "For you" ranks drops by how new they are, how people respond to them (likes, shares, views watched to the end), the distance of the store from your delivery address when you share it, and the store's rating. We do not build a personal profile for it. "Latest" shows drops newest first, with no ranking. You can switch at any time (Digital Services Act Art. 27).


Delivery jobs are offered to approved, online couriers within 7.5 km of the store, nearest first. Couriers choose which jobs to accept. No job is assigned automatically, and no decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made solely by automated means (GDPR Art. 22). Couriers can ask a person, through Support in the courier app or the contact form at https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact, to explain or review any decision.

## 5. Who receives your data

- **The store** you buy from: the items ordered, the delivery address with its map pin and your order notes. The store does not get your name, email address or phone number; for a pickup order it gets no address at all, and you show a pickup code instead.
- **Your courier:** your name, the delivery address with its map pin and your order notes, but only after they accept the job. Before that, couriers see only the approximate area (about 1 km).
- **Customers of an order:** the store's name, address and phone number, and the courier's name and live position during the delivery (from the courier's acceptance until delivery; not afterwards).
- **Everyone (public):** store profiles (section 2), reviews and drop comments with the writer's first name, live-chat messages with the writer's first name.
- **Service providers (processors)** under data-processing agreements:
  - Stripe Payments Europe (payments; payouts to stores and couriers through Stripe Connect)
  - Cloudinary (storing and delivering photos, videos and sound)
  - Google Firebase (sign-in incl. phone and Apple sign-in, push notifications, Crashlytics crash reports) and Google Sign-In
  - Render (hosting and database, Frankfurt)
  - Twilio (SMS codes, sent only to Finnish +358 numbers, and withdrawal acknowledgements for accounts that have only a phone number)
  - LiveKit (live-stream video), only while live shopping is switched on
  - Sentry (error reports), only if switched on
  - Have I Been Pwned (Cloudflare): receives only a 5-character hash prefix of a new password (k-anonymity), never the password
  - [Redis provider] (shared counters, queues and caches, no customer content), only if switched on
  - [EMAIL PROVIDER] (email), and [MAILBOX PROVIDER] for the team mailbox that receives support requests
- **OpenStreetMap Foundation** (an independent recipient, not our processor): the apps load map pictures from tile.openstreetmap.org and look up addresses at nominatim.openstreetmap.org directly from your phone.
- **The Finnish Patent and Registration Office (PRH)**, for business sellers only: we look up the business ID in its free open data service (avoindata.prh.fi). PRH receives only the business ID, not who asked.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it (for example the Finnish Tax Administration for DAC7 seller reporting).

**Seller tax details (DAC7).** A seller's tax identification number is stored encrypted (AES-256) and is shown to the seller only in part (its last characters). It is decrypted only for Malvoya's yearly report, which only Malvoya's administrators can open, and it is sent only to the Finnish Tax Administration; that authority may share it with the tax authorities of other EU countries where the seller lives, as the directive provides. Sellers who stay under the directive's threshold (fewer than 30 sales and at most 2 000 € in a year) are not reported, but their details are kept as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can:
- **access** your data and **export** it in a machine-readable file (Malvoya app: Profile → Privacy → Your data → Download your data). The Malvoya Store app (Store tab → Download my data) and the Malvoya Courier app (Account → Download my data) have the same button from version 1.9.1; on older versions ask through Help in the app or the contact form, and we send you the same file (for couriers it includes your jobs with their pay breakdown, the ratings you got and your payouts; for stores the payouts);
- **correct** your data (your name in the app's profile settings; your email address, phone number or anything else through Help or the contact form);
- **delete** your account (Profile → Privacy → Your data → Delete my account; how in each app: [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/delete-account](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/delete-account)). Order records we must keep by law are anonymised rather than deleted. Your sign-in record at Google Firebase and, for a store, its photos and videos at Cloudinary are deleted too;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest (for example crash reports);
- **restrict** processing;
- **withdraw consent** where we asked for it: cancel the "tell me when Malvoya opens near me" alert or switch optional emails off (Profile → Privacy → Your data → Email preferences, or the unsubscribe link in the email).

Use the contact form at https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact (topic "Privacy (GDPR) request") or Support in the app. We reply within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutetun toimisto)**, tietosuoja.fi, PL 800, 00531 Helsinki.

## 9. Security

- Passwords are hashed with bcrypt.
- Sessions use short-lived tokens (15 minutes) and single-use refresh tokens, which are stored only as hashes. Sign-in and password-reset codes are stored only as hashes; they and pickup codes stop working after a few wrong tries.
- All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
- Sellers' tax identification numbers and administrators' two-step sign-in secrets are encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
- Delivery photos are private and opened only through short-lived signed links; statement and agreement-copy links are signed and expire after 10 minutes; unsubscribe links are signed.
- Sign-in, password reset, support and other sensitive actions are rate-limited.
- Administrators must use two-step sign-in, and changes they make to commissions, legal texts, recalls, live streams and two-step settings, approvals of stores and couriers, courier suspensions and bans, and every opening or download of the DAC7 report (when tax identification numbers are decrypted) are recorded in an audit log.
- Access to live order data is limited to the customer, store and courier of that order.
- The database is backed up by our hosting provider (point-in-time recovery).

## 11. Changes

Every change to this policy is published as a new version with a summary of what changed and the date it takes effect. We tell you by push notification and email, in your language, before the change takes effect (or at the latest when it does, if the law requires a change at once). Every version, with its dates, stays readable at [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/privacy/versions](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/privacy/versions). The app asks you to confirm you have read the new version; this is an acknowledgement, not consent, and it never blocks you from using your account, exporting your data or deleting it.
