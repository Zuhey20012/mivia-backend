# Copyright and trademark notices / Tekijänoikeus- ja tavaramerkki-ilmoitukset

Malvoya is operated by [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (Y-tunnus [Y-TUNNUS]) ("Malvoya"), a marketplace for shoppers in Finland. This page explains how holders of copyright or trademark rights can tell us about infringing content, how sellers can respond, and what happens to accounts that infringe again and again.

Malvoya handles these notices under the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, "DSA", Articles 16, 17, 20, 21 and 23) and Finnish law. The US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) does not apply to Malvoya; you do not need a DMCA-style notice, but a notice that contains the information below is handled in the same way whatever it is called.

## 1. What you can report

- **Copyright:** a product photo, video, drop, sound, description or comment that uses your work (for example your photograph, design drawing, text or music) without your permission.
- **Trademark:** a product, listing or drop that uses your trade mark without permission, including counterfeit goods.

Other illegal content (for example unsafe products) is reported with the other report reasons.

## 2. How to report

- **In the app:** open the product, drop or comment, choose **Report** and then **Copyright** or **Trademark**. This works without an account for products and drops; comments are reported by signed-in users.
- **Without the app:** use the contact form at [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact) (topic "Illegal content") and include the information below.

A notice must contain (DSA Art. 16(2)):

- your **name** and **email address** (and, if you act for the rights holder, whom you act for);
- the **work or mark** concerned, with enough detail to identify it (for a registered trade mark, the registration number and register, for example EUIPO or PRH);
- the **exact location** of the content on Malvoya (the link, or the product, drop or comment and where on it);
- an **explanation** of why the content infringes your rights;
- a **statement** that you believe in good faith that the information and allegations in the notice are accurate and complete.

The app asks for each of these and does not send a copyright or trademark report without them.

## 3. What happens next

- A person on the Malvoya team reviews every notice. We may ask you for more information or evidence of your rights.
- We decide without undue delay. If the content infringes, we remove it or make it unavailable (a listing is locked and cannot be republished; a drop or comment is removed).
- **The seller or author** gets a statement of reasons (DSA Art. 17): what was removed, why, that it was decided by a person after a notice, and how to object. We tell them what was reported and why, but **not your name or email** unless you agree or the law requires it. ## 4. For sellers: responding to a notice (counter-notice)

If your listing, drop or comment was removed after a copyright or trademark notice and you believe this was a mistake (for example you own the photo, have a licence, or sell genuine goods you bought lawfully):

- reply to the statement of reasons, or use Support in the app or the contact form at [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact) (topic "Seller complaint"), **within six months** of the decision;
- explain why you have the right to use the content and attach evidence (licence, invoice from an authorised supplier, original photo files);
- a person who did not make the first decision reviews your complaint free of charge (DSA Art. 20) and restores the content if the removal was not justified. You are told the result and the reasons.

You can also use a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body (DSA Art. 21) or go to court. In Finland, copyright and trademark disputes belong to the Market Court (markkinaoikeus). [LAWYER: confirm.]

The "I have the right to use these photos and texts" confirmation that sellers give before importing a catalogue is a binding statement under the Seller Terms; sellers remain responsible for what they upload.

## 5. Repeat infringers

Following DSA Art. 23:

- A seller or user whose content is removed after an upheld copyright or trademark notice receives a warning with each statement of reasons.
- After **[3] upheld notices within [12] months**, Malvoya suspends the account's ability to publish products, drops or comments for a reasonable period, after a prior warning. A seller whose infringement continues may have its agreement ended under the Seller Terms (section 8). [LAWYER: confirm the thresholds and the suspension period.]
- People who often send **manifestly unfounded** notices may, after a warning, have their notices left unprocessed for a reasonable period.
- Each decision is made by a person, considers the circumstances (how many items, how serious, whether in good faith) and comes with reasons and a way to object.

## 6. Contact

Notices, counter-notices and questions: [malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact](https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/contact), which is also Malvoya's DSA point of contact.

## Suomeksi

Tekijänoikeuden tai tavaramerkin haltija voi ilmoittaa Malvoyassa olevasta loukkaavasta sisällöstä sovelluksessa (Ilmoita → Tekijänoikeus / Tavaramerkki) tai yhteydenottolomakkeella (aihe "Laiton sisältö"). Ilmoituksessa on oltava nimesi ja sähköpostiosoitteesi, kyseinen teos tai tavaramerkki, sisällön tarkka sijainti, selitys loukkauksesta ja vakuutus siitä, että tiedot ovat vilpittömässä mielessä oikeat ja täydelliset (DSA 16 artikla). Ihminen käsittelee jokaisen ilmoituksen. Jos sisältö poistetaan, myyjä saa perustelut (DSA 17 artikla) ja voi hakea päätökseen muutosta kuuden kuukauden kuluessa maksutta (DSA 20 artikla). Toistuvasti loukkaavien tilien julkaisuoikeus voidaan varoituksen jälkeen keskeyttää (DSA 23 artikla). Yhdysvaltain DMCA-laki ei koske Malvoyaa. [LAKIMIES: tarkista suomenkielinen teksti.]
