# Malvoya Courier Agreement / Lähettisopimus

**Last updated:** [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

> **Before publishing:** have this agreement reviewed by a Finnish employment lawyer. Under the EU Platform Work Directive (2024/2831, to be applied from 2 December 2026), a courier is presumed to be an employee when the platform directs and controls the work. The terms below are designed so that couriers genuinely control their work. The way the service is run in practice must match them.

## 1. Parties

[COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (Y-tunnus [Y-TUNNUS]) ("Malvoya") and the courier named in the app ("Courier").

## 2. Nature of the relationship

The Courier is an independent entrepreneur. The Courier decides:
- **when** to be online;
- **which jobs** to accept (every job can be declined without consequence);
- **how** to perform deliveries.

The Courier may work for other platforms. The Courier needs a Y-tunnus (or a light-entrepreneur service) and handles their own taxes and YEL insurance where applicable.

## 3. Onboarding

Before approval, Malvoya checks:
- the Courier's identity;
- their right to work in Finland;
- that they are at least 18 years old.

Documents are checked through [VERIFICATION METHOD], not collected in the app.

## 4. How jobs are offered (transparency of automated systems)

- Jobs are offered to approved, online couriers within 7.5 km of the store, nearest first. If none is nearby, the job is shown to all online couriers.
- The only factor used is distance to the store. Ratings, acceptance rates and past declines are **not** used.
- Every offer shows the pickup store, the approximate delivery area and the Courier's pay for the job, with a line-by-line explanation of how it was calculated, before the Courier accepts.
- Malvoya does not use automated systems to suspend or terminate couriers. Any such decision is made by a person, with written reasons, and the Courier may request a review.

## 5. Fees and payment

- The pay for each job is calculated by a published formula and shown, with its breakdown, before acceptance. It is set separately from the delivery fee the customer pays. The formula is:
  - a **base amount** per delivery (pickup and handover);
  - plus an amount **per kilometre** from the store to the customer. The distance is the straight-line distance multiplied by a fixed road factor and rounded to 0.1 km;
  - with a **minimum** for the base amount and distance together;
  - plus an amount per kilometre for the **ride to the store** beyond a free distance, measured from the Courier's last shared location when they accept the job to the store (straight line × the same road factor). The free distance is not paid separately;
  - plus **waiting time at the store**: when the Courier checks in at the store in the app, waiting until pickup is measured; whole minutes beyond a number of free minutes are paid per minute, up to a maximum per job. Waiting pay is added at pickup;
  - plus any **tip**.
- The current values of these parameters are [COURIER PAY PARAMETERS: base amount, per-km rate, road factor, minimum, ride-to-store free distance and rate, free waiting minutes, per-minute rate and maximum] and are always shown in the app in one sentence before a job is accepted. Malvoya gives at least [NOTICE PERIOD] notice before changing them. The rates in force when a job is offered apply to that whole job, including ride-to-store and waiting pay; a later change never reduces the pay of a job already offered or accepted.
- The same formula applies to every Courier. Ratings, acceptance rates and past declines do not affect pay.
- Tips: customers can add an optional tip at checkout. Malvoya passes every tip to the Courier in full and takes no commission on it. A tip shown in an offer is already included in the pay shown.
- Each completed delivery is paid to the Courier's own Stripe account right after delivery (Stripe Connect). Stripe verifies the Courier's identity and bank account on its own pages.
- The Courier is responsible for their own taxes and insurance as an independent contractor.
- Each job's final pay breakdown stays visible in the app. Malvoya provides a monthly statement of completed jobs and pay.

## 6. Location data

The Courier's location is processed only while they are online, as described in the Privacy Policy. On Android a permanent notification shows whenever the location is being shared. Only the latest location is kept, and it is deleted when the Courier goes offline. The location at the moment the Courier accepts a job is used once to calculate the pay for the ride to the store (section 5); only the resulting distance is stored with the job. When an order is left at the door, the Courier takes a delivery photo; it is visible only to the customer and Malvoya and deleted after 30 days.

## 7. Conduct and safety

The Courier follows traffic law and treats customers and stores respectfully. The customer's address and door codes are used only to complete the delivery.

## 8. Ending the agreement

- The Courier may stop at any time.
- Malvoya may end the agreement with [14] days' notice, or immediately for serious breach, always with written reasons and the option of a review.

## 9. Law and disputes

This agreement is governed by Finnish law. Disputes go to [Helsinki District Court].
