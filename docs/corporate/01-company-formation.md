# 01 — Setting up Malvoya Oy alone

> **Not legal, tax or accounting advice.** Preparation material for a Finnish lawyer and accountant, written 3 November 2026. Statute text was read on 3 November 2026 from Finlex open data (Limited Liability Companies Act, osakeyhtiölaki 624/2006, consolidated version `fin@20240652`, the latest Finnish consolidation listed by Finlex that day; later amendments, if any, are not reflected). Fees and processing times were read on prh.fi and ytj.fi the same day. Everything in `[BRACKETS]` must be filled by the founder; nothing here is a real name, address or number.

## 1. What an Oy needs, in one paragraph

A private limited company (yksityinen osakeyhtiö, Oy) is founded by signing a **founding agreement** (perustamissopimus) that has the **articles of association** (yhtiöjärjestys) attached, subscribing for the shares, and filing a **start-up notification** (perustamisilmoitus) with the Finnish Patent and Registration Office (Patentti- ja rekisterihallitus, PRH) within **three months** of signing, or the founding lapses (ch. 2 §8). The company exists as a legal person only once it is entered in the trade register (kaupparekisteri). One person may be the only shareholder and the only ordinary board member, but then the board must also have a **deputy member** (ch. 6 §8). A managing director is optional (ch. 6 §1: "Sillä voi olla myös toimitusjohtaja").

## 2. The founding agreement (perustamissopimus)

### 2.1 What the law requires it to contain (ch. 2 §2, read 3 Nov 2026)

1. The date of the agreement.
2. Every shareholder and the shares each subscribes.
3. The amount paid to the company for a share (subscription price, merkintähinta).
4. The payment period for the shares (maksuaika).
5. The members of the board.
6. The articles of association, included or attached (ch. 2 §3).
7. The financial period (tilikausi), either here or in the articles.
8. Where needed: the managing director and the auditors.
9. Optionally: the chair of the board.

The subscription price goes to share capital unless the agreement or the articles direct it, in whole or in part, to the **reserve for invested unrestricted equity** (sijoitetun vapaan oman pääoman rahasto, SVOP) (ch. 2 §4). Directing it to SVOP keeps share capital at 0 € while still putting money in, and SVOP money can later be returned to shareholders more flexibly than share capital (accountant to confirm the tax treatment).

### 2.3 Share subscription

For a single founder the founding agreement **is** the subscription (ch. 2 §2 item 2). No separate subscription list (merkintälista) is needed at founding. Shares subscribed later (investors, a share issue, osakeanti, ch. 9) need a separate board or shareholders' decision and subscription.

**How many shares?** Issue many (for example 100 000 A shares at 0 € or 0,01 €). It costs nothing and makes later splits, option pools and small percentages easy. The articles in 02 have no nominal value (nimellisarvo); Finnish shares need none.

### 2.4 Shareholder register (osakasluettelo)

When the shares are not in the book-entry system, the board must keep a **shareholder register** with each shareholder's name and address and the shares they hold (ch. 3 §15, read 3 Nov 2026). A new owner cannot use shareholder rights before being entered in it or having notified the company of the acquisition (ch. 3 §2). Keep it as a dated document in the company archive, update it at every transfer or issue, and keep a scan with the minutes. Fields: shareholder name, personal identity code or business ID, address, email for notices, share numbers or quantity, share class (A/B), date entered, restrictions (redemption and consent clauses), pledges.

### 2.5 Payment of share capital (only if not 0 €)

1. Ask a bank for an account for a company being founded (perustettavan yhtiön tili). Banks may require the signed founding agreement and identification of the founder and the beneficial owner.
2. Pay the subscription price by the deadline in the founding agreement.
3. Keep the bank statement or receipt: it proves payment for the registration declaration. An auditor's certificate (ch. 2 §8) is required only in the cases the Act lists (for example payment in kind); lawyer to confirm for Malvoya's case.

**Recommendation for the lawyer to confirm:** start with **0 €** share capital, or a small amount booked to SVOP. The company's real starting capital can be loaned or invested by the founder later (keep a written loan agreement for any founder loan).

## 3. Own the shares directly or through a holding company?

| | Direct ownership | Personal holding company (holding-yhtiö) |
|---|---|---|
| Set-up | One company | Two companies (two notifications, two sets of books) |
| Cost per year | One accountant | Roughly double the accounting and filing |
| Exit / dividends | Taxed personally when received | Dividends between Finnish companies are often tax-free and gains can be reinvested before personal tax; complex rules |
| Control | The same | The same (the holding owns the A shares; the founder owns the holding) |

**For the accountant:** whether a holding company is worth it now or only before a large investment round. If added later, moving shares into it is a taxable event in some cases, so decide before value grows. The articles in 02 let A shares move to a founder-controlled holding company without losing their votes.

## 4. The board: a sole member and a deputy

### 4.1 The rules (read 3 Nov 2026)

- The board has **1–5 ordinary members** unless the articles say otherwise. **If it has fewer than three members, it must have at least one deputy member** (ch. 6 §8).
- A board member (and a deputy) must be a **natural person**; not a minor, not under guardianship with limited capacity, not bankrupt, and not subject to a business prohibition (liiketoimintakielto) (ch. 6 §10).
- **At least one board member must live in the European Economic Area**, unless PRH grants an exemption (ch. 6 §10). You living in Finland satisfies this.
- Board members are elected by the general meeting (ch. 6 §9). Whoever elected a member can **remove** them at any time (ch. 6 §13: "Hallituksen jäsenen voi ennen toimikauden päättymistä erottaa se, joka on hänet valinnut"). As the only shareholder you can replace the deputy at any time by a written shareholder decision.
- In a private company the term is **indefinite** unless the articles say otherwise (ch. 6 §11).

### 4.2 Who can be the deputy

Any adult who meets ch. 6 §10. In practice: a parent, sibling, partner, close friend, or a trusted mentor or business adviser. Accounting firms and lawyers sometimes accept the role for a fee, but many refuse because of liability.

### 4.3 What the deputy does, and the risks

- **Role:** the deputy steps in only when the ordinary member cannot act (for example illness, disqualification due to a conflict of interest, or death). The conflict case comes up at once: a board member may not handle a contract between themself and the company (ch. 6 §4), so contracts between you and Malvoya Oy (the IP assignment in 03, a founder loan, your pay) are decided and signed on the company's side by the deputy, unless the lawyer chooses another route. While stepping in, the deputy has the full powers of a board member: they can sign for the company and make board decisions.
- **Risk for you (control):** a deputy who acts outside that role, or who refuses to cooperate when needed (for example when you are ill), can stall the company. Choose someone you trust and who is reachable. You can remove and replace them by a shareholder decision.
- **Risk for the deputy (liability):** a board member is liable for damage caused by breaching the Companies Act or the articles intentionally or negligently (ch. 22). A deputy who never acts carries little practical risk, but they become personally responsible when they do act (for example for unpaid taxes in some cases, or for a breach of duty). They are also listed publicly in the trade register with their name, and PRH needs their identification data. Tell them this in writing.
- **Recommended paperwork (for the lawyer):** a short letter of consent from the deputy (suostumus hallituksen varajäseneksi), a one-page "deputy instructions" note (when to act, who to call, where the credentials safe is: see 03 §6), and, once the company can afford it, D&O insurance (08).

### 4.4 Alternative: a three-member board

Three ordinary members need no deputy. It brings more people into decisions, which reduces control; it is normal after a seed round (see 04 §2), not for a sole founder.

## 5. The managing director (toimitusjohtaja)

Optional (ch. 6 §1). If there is one, the **board** appoints and dismisses them and sets their pay (ch. 6 §20), and they run day-to-day business.

| | No managing director | Founder as managing director |
|---|---|---|
| Paperwork | Less | One more registration entry |
| YEL / employment | Board member owning over 30 % alone is a YEL entrepreneur anyway | Same: a managing director owning over 30 % alone is a YEL entrepreneur, not an employee for pension purposes |
| Investors | Usually ask for one later | Expected |
| Recommendation | Start without, add later (simple change notification) | When the first hire or investor arrives |

## 6. The start-up notification (perustamisilmoitus) at ytj.fi

### 6.1 Channel, fee and timing (read 3 Nov 2026)

- Since **1 January 2026**, an Oy cannot file on paper; notifications are made online at **ytj.fi**, in Finnish or Swedish, with strong electronic identification (prh.fi, "Start a limited liability company"). The form names below follow the paper Y1 form's instructions, because the online service asks for the same data.
- Two online routes (prh.fi):
  - **Alternative A — guided set-up package: 300 €.** The service writes the founding agreement and **standard articles**. Conditions: share capital 0 €, no subscription price, only adult shareholders, Finnish personal identity codes for everyone filed.
  - **Alternative B — start-up notification with your own documents: 400 €.** Needed if there is share capital, a subscription price, corporate shareholders, people without a Finnish identity code, or **articles beyond the standard ones** (A/B share classes, redemption and consent clauses).
- The fee is paid in the online service after signing.
- **Processing:** PRH's processing-time page (status 2 Nov 2026) was handling online start-up notifications that arrived on **27 Oct 2026**, i.e. about one week. The page is updated weekly.

### 6.4 Beneficial owners (tosiasialliset edunsaajat)

- Who: a natural person who ultimately owns, directly or indirectly, **more than 25 %** of the shares, or otherwise exercises control (Anti-Money Laundering Act 444/2017, ch. 1 §5, read from Finlex 3 Nov 2026). With a sole founder: **you**, owning 100 % directly (or through your holding company).
- Fields: name, personal identity code, nationality, country of residence, nature of control (ownership share / votes), date from which.
- Filed in the same start-up notification. PRH's 2026 fee announcement lists no separate fee for it; confirm in the service.
- **Update without delay** whenever ownership changes (each investment round). PRH can order a company into liquidation for missing filings (yrittajat.fi news, cited in the 3 Nov 2026 search; lawyer to confirm the current sanction).

### 6.5 Employer register — only when employing

Do not tick it now. When employing: tick it with the date of regular pay; before the first pay day arrange **statutory accident insurance** (työtapaturma- ja ammattitautivakuutus), **TyEL pension insurance** and **unemployment insurance** (via the Employment Fund), and report pay to the **Incomes Register** (tulorekisteri) within 5 days of payment (see 06).

## 7. After registration: first-week checklist

1. Download the trade register extract (kaupparekisteriote) and the articles from virre.prh.fi; save in the company archive.
2. First **board meeting** minutes (ch. 6 §6: minutes signed by the chair; with a sole member, by that member): accept the IP assignment (03), open the bank account, appoint signatories, approve the accountant, approve account transfers, adopt the credentials policy (03 §6).
3. Open the bank account (company KYC: extract, articles, ID, beneficial owners).
4. Engage the accountant; send them this folder.
5. YEL within 6 months (if it applies).
6. D-U-N-S request with the exact registered name and address.
7. Update `legal/*.md` placeholders (07) and publish.
