# Google Play store listings

Copy for the three listings, in English (default) and Finnish (add Finnish under **Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing → Manage translations → Finnish**).

Limits (Play Console): title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Every text below was counted with a script on 2026-10-15; the counts are in brackets.

Rules this copy follows (Google Play metadata policy): no emoji, no ALL CAPS, nothing like "best", "#1", "top", "new", "free", "% off" in the title; no invented numbers, ratings, awards, testimonials or partner logos. Malvoya is new, and the copy says so. Every feature named is in the current code.

**Full description** [2275]
```text
Malvoya is a marketplace for local fashion in Finland. Shop clothes, accessories and second-hand finds from independent stores near you. A local courier picks up your order from the shop and brings it to your door, or you collect it yourself.

Malvoya is new. We are opening area by area, so there may be only a few stores near you at first. If there are none yet, tap "Notify me when it opens here" and we will send you one message when a store opens near you.

SHOP STORES NEAR YOU
- See stores near your delivery address, with distance, delivery fee and whether they are open right now.
- Each store page shows who the seller is and whether it is a business or a private seller.
- Products have photos, and some have short videos. Sizes and options show what is in stock.
- Save favourites and follow the stores you like.

DROPS
- Drops are short videos and photos posted by stores.
- "For you" orders them by how new they are, how people react, distance and store rating. We do not build a profile of you. "Latest" shows the newest first, and "Following" shows the stores you follow.
- You can comment, and report anything that breaks the rules.

CLEAR PRICES
- You see the full price before you pay: items, delivery fee, any other fees and any discount code.
- Payment is handled by Stripe. Malvoya never sees your card number.
- You can add a tip for the courier. The whole tip goes to the courier.

DELIVERY OR PICKUP
- Delivery: follow your courier on a map and message them about the delivery.
- Reserve & collect: pick the order up at the shop with a code. No delivery fee.
- The store has a few minutes to accept your order. If it does not, or no courier is found, the order is cancelled and refunded automatically.

RETURNS AND REVIEWS
- From business sellers you can withdraw from a purchase within 14 days of receiving it. Start the return in the app, for the whole order or some items.
- Only customers whose order was delivered can review a store.

YOUR DATA
- Download your data or delete your account in the app: Profile > Privacy.
- No ads and no ad tracking.

Malvoya is for people aged 18 or over. The app is in Finnish and English.

Privacy policy: https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/privacy
```

```text
Malvoya Store is the app for independent fashion shops and second-hand sellers on Malvoya, a local marketplace in Finland. Customers nearby find your products, and local couriers deliver the orders, or customers collect them from your shop.

Malvoya is new and opening area by area, so order volumes will be small at first.

GET STARTED
- Create your store: name, category, pickup address, logo and banner.
- Choose whether you sell as a business (with a Y-tunnus) or as a private person. Shoppers see this.
- Our team reviews each store before shoppers can see it.

PRODUCTS
- Add products with photos, prices, sizes or other options and stock.

- Post short videos and photos to the Drops feed, linked to your products.
- People who follow your store get a notification when a drop goes live.
- Reply to comments on your drops.

ORDERS
- New paid orders ring on your phone until you answer.
- Accept or decline. An order that is not answered within 10 minutes is cancelled and refunded, so keep notifications on.
- Mark an order ready. A courier collects it, or the customer collects it with a pickup code that you check in the app.
- Set opening hours, or pause new orders with one switch.
- Handle returns and refunds in the app.

GETTING PAID
- Payouts go to your bank account through Stripe Connect. You set up your account and verify your identity on Stripe's own pages.
- The commission is shown in the app and in the seller terms before you sell.
- A store payout is released after delivery and the return period, and is held while a return is open.

Seller terms: https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/sellers
```

## 3. Courier app — `com.malvoya.courier`

### English

**Title** [28]
```text
Malvoya Courier – Deliveries
```

**Short description** [70]
```text
Deliver local orders when it suits you. See the pay before you accept.
```

**Full description** [2190]
```text
Malvoya Courier is the app for independent couriers who deliver orders from local fashion shops to customers on Malvoya, a local marketplace in Finland.

Malvoya is new and opening area by area. Jobs may be rare at first.

HOW IT WORKS
- Sign up in the app. Our team contacts you to check your identity, that you are at least 18 and that you have the right to work in Finland. No documents are uploaded in the app.
- You work as an independent entrepreneur under the courier agreement. There are no shifts: go online when it suits you and go offline when you are done.
- While you are online, job offers come from stores within 7.5 km, nearest first. If no courier is near, a job is shown to all online couriers. You choose which jobs to accept. Nothing is assigned automatically.

PAY YOU CAN CHECK
- Every offer shows your pay for that job before you accept, with a line-by-line explanation of how it was calculated.
- Pay can include a base fee, distance, the ride to the store, waiting at the store and tips. Customers' tips go to you in full.
- Ratings, acceptance rates and declines are not used to decide which jobs you are offered.
- Payouts go to your bank account through Stripe Connect. You set up your account on Stripe's own pages.

ON THE JOB
- Step by step: go to the store, check in, pick up, deliver.
- Message the customer about the delivery.
- Hand the order over in person, or leave it at the door and take a photo of the parcel. Only the customer sees the photo, and it is deleted after 30 days.

YOUR LOCATION
Malvoya Courier collects your precise location while you are online, including when the app is in the background or the screen is off. It is used to offer you nearby jobs, check that you are at the store, calculate your pay for the ride to the store, and show your position to the customer and the store of the order you are delivering. A notification shows the whole time. Sharing stops when you go offline, and your stored position is then deleted. Your location is not used for ads.

Courier agreement: https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/couriers
Privacy policy: https://malvoya-api-eu.onrender.com/legal/privacy

The app is in Finnish and English.
```
